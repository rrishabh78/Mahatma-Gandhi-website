About Mahatma Gandhi

Mohandas Karamchand Gandhi was one of the most influential leaders of India's freedom movement. He fought against British rule through truth, non-violence and peaceful resistance. His life continues to inspire people around the world.

📅 Important Events and Years

Explore the major milestones in Gandhi's life.

1869

Birth of Mahatma Gandhi

Born on 2 October in Porbandar, Gujarat.

1888

Journey to England

Went to England to study law.

1893

South Africa

Travelled to South Africa for legal work and began opposing racial discrimination.

1915

Return to India

Returned to India from South Africa on 9 January.

1917

Champaran Satyagraha

Led a movement in Bihar to support indigo farmers against unjust practices.

1918

Kheda and Ahmedabad Movements

Supported farmers in Kheda and mill workers in Ahmedabad.

1920

Non-Cooperation Movement

Launched a nationwide movement against British colonial rule.

1930

Dandi March

Started the Salt March on 12 March and broke the salt law at Dandi on 6 April.

1931

Gandhi-Irwin Pact

The agreement between Gandhi and Lord Irwin was announced on 5 March.

1942

Quit India Movement

The Quit India resolution was adopted on 8 August. Gandhi gave the call "Do or Die."

1947

Indian Independence

India became independent on 15 August. Gandhi worked to promote communal harmony.

1948

Death of Mahatma Gandhi

He was assassinated in New Delhi on 30 January.

📚 Important Notes for Students

01. Satyagraha

Satyagraha means resisting injustice through truth, non-violence and peaceful action.

02. Non-Violence

Gandhi believed that conflicts should be resolved without violence.

03. Swadeshi

Swadeshi encouraged the use of Indian-made goods and promoted khadi.

04. Champaran Satyagraha

In 1917, Gandhi supported indigo farmers in Champaran, Bihar.

05. Dandi March

In 1930, Gandhi led a march from Sabarmati Ashram to Dandi to protest the salt tax.

06. Quit India Movement

Launched in 1942, this movement demanded an end to British rule in India.
